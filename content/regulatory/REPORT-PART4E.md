## New mandatory training and qualification duties confirmed (selection)

- **Pakistan:** board-approved FX training policy with a yearly training-needs assessment per employee (FX Manual
  Ch. 2); trained FX customer-service staff; qualified agri-finance staff; microfinance collection staff training;
  branchless banking agent training (AML/CFT); remittance agent and e-PRC training.
- **United States:** Bank Protection Act security training (12 CFR 208.61); Regulation CC employee procedures
  (229.19(f)); certified/licensed appraisers (225.66); Volcker Rule training for trading staff (248.20).
- **United Kingdom:** market abuse surveillance training (Delegated Regulation 2016/957 Art 4); mortgage adviser
  qualifications (FCA TC).
- **UAE:** qualified staff for regulatory reporting (Federal Decree-Law 6/2025 Art 122).
- **Australia:** credit reporting staff training (Credit Reporting Code 2025); trained and competent credit
  representatives; market integrity rules qualifications; Consumer Data Right staff security training before access
  and annually.
- **India:** certificate course before posting to treasury, risk management, accounting/audit and credit roles (RBI
  Miscellaneous Directions paras 59-60); counterfeit-detection training for all cash staff; card agent and call-centre
  training; fraud-staff training; trained officials for video-KYC.
- **Bangladesh:** annual risk conference and risk training; credit approver training and assessment test; trained
  treasury dealers; SME and agri credit officer training; banknote identification in the annual programme; card,
  MFS and agent-banking training; IFRS 9 ECL training.
- **Malaysia:** board risk education; credit risk officer training and CPD; trained liquidity officers; card
  front-line and sales training (2025 card policy documents); money services staff and agent training; SC
  representative CPE; trade surveillance and wholesale market code training.
- **Indonesia:** risk management training for business, risk and audit units; payment-system competency certificate
  within 6 months (PBI 5/2024); treasury dealer certification (PBI 6/2024, PADG 17/2026); LKD and Laku Pandai agent
  training.
- **Saudi Arabia:** credit staff training (SAMA Credit Risk Rules); consumer finance staff training (Art 15);
  qualified real estate finance advisers; teller and customer-service courses before assignment (Account Opening
  Rule 9); agent banking training incl. the Retail Banking Professional Foundation Certificate; remittance centre
  training.
- **Qatar:** board induction and continuing training (QCB Circular 2/2023).
- **Bahrain:** risk function staff qualified and trained (Module HC-7).
- **Canada:** Bank Act s 627.02 training extended to consumer credit, account opening and cheque holds; FCAC
  guideline training for mortgage relief and branch closure staff; Payment Card Code training; competent IFRS 9 staff.
- **South Africa:** fraud and financial crime training (Banks Regulation 50); insider trading and market abuse;
  payment-system cyber training (NPS Directive 1/2024); exchange and CSD participant qualification standards.
