## The two final quests

- **Pakistan, `pk-sbp-fx-remittance-branchless`** (main program 152, mandatory-role): SBP Foreign Exchange Manual
  Ch. 2, 10, 12-13, Branchless Banking Regulations and wire-transfer information under the AML/CFT/CPF Regulations,
  all from SBP PDFs opened in full. Linked from programs 142, 150, 151, 152, 153. Trade finance (186) is only
  introduced, so it is not linked.
- **UAE, `ae-cbuae-law-2025`** (main program 175, mandatory-role): Federal Decree-Law No. 6 of 2025, read in full
  from the Ministry of Finance copy (the CBUAE sites did not respond). Linked from 12 programs; the Consumer
  Protection Regulation (51) and the ombudsman (60) are not linked because their own texts could not be opened.

Both use fictional banks and say so; no real enforcement case could be verified from an official page.
