# Global Banking Regulatory Training — Phase 1 creation report (Part 1)

Reviewed: 8 November 2026. Scope: Pakistan, United States, United Kingdom, UAE, Singapore, Australia.

## How accuracy was handled

1. Research agents mapped each country against the 202-program catalogue from official domains only.
2. A second, independent pass opened the official texts and confirmed, corrected or downgraded every entry.
   References whose text was read carry `checked: 2026-11-08`; the admin library shows them with ✓.
3. Several official sites block automated access (State Bank of Pakistan HTML pages, CBUAE Rulebook,
   uaelegislation.gov.ae, eCFR, FFIEC, Singapore Statutes Online, AUSTRAC, OAIC, ICO). Entries resting only on
   such sources are marked **⏳ source check pending** in the admin library: open the link and confirm before
   relying on them. No quest fact was taken from an unchecked source.
4. The quest writers used only checked references or official texts they opened themselves; anything else is
   described in general terms, without numbers.

stage 1 free, XP/badges/leaderboard as for every quest; downloadable stage slides.

HSBC 2021, Starling 2024, Monzo 2025, Barclays 2025); FinCEN/OFAC actions (LaFontaine 2020, USAA 2022, Shinhan
