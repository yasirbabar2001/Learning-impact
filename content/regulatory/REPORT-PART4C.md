Reviewed: 8 November 2026. Nine quests, one per Phase 2 country, written only from official texts that were opened.
All are drafts until an admin prices and publishes them.

## Corrections applied to the maps

- India: RBI's Cybersecurity Directions para 204 also require annual training for all Board members and senior
  management (program 71 updated).
- Indonesia: program 72 (cyber awareness) moved to competence — the yearly awareness and phishing simulation sit in
  SEOJK 29/2022's maturity-assessment criteria, not its binding text; POJK 11/2022 took effect three months after
  promulgation (effective date corrected).
