## Key training obligations confirmed in official texts

- **India:** RBI KYC Directions 2025 para 79 (ongoing KYC/AML/CFT training by role); RBI Cybersecurity Directions
  2026 paras 199-204 (awareness for all new recruits, annual for management and the Board; incidents to RBI within
  6 hours); RBI Compliance Function Directions 2026 para 52; RBI Miscellaneous Directions para 59 (certification before
  posting to treasury, risk, credit and third-party sales).
- **Bangladesh:** BFIU Circular 26 para 11.2; MLP Rules 2019 rule 6(3); BB ICT Security Guideline v4.0 (on joining and
  yearly); Cybersecurity Framework 2026; Labour (Amendment) Act 2026 (violence and harassment training).
- **Malaysia:** AMLA s 19(2)(b); BNM AML/CFT/CPF policy document paras 11.6.1-11.6.7; RMiT (annual cybersecurity
  awareness for all staff); FTFC, MCIPD and Complaints Handling policy documents; SC CPE (20 points a year).
- **Indonesia:** POJK 8/2023 (AML/CFT/CPF training at least yearly, employee screening); POJK 22/2023 (consumer
  protection training); POJK 12/2024 (anti-fraud education yearly); SEOJK 28/2022 (risk management certification);
  SEOJK 29/2022 (annual cyber awareness and phishing simulation).
- **Saudi Arabia:** AML Law Implementing Regulation Art 14/1(e); CTF Implementing Regulations Art 18(1)(f); SAMA
  AML/CTF Guide s 11; Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules (Dec 2025); Principles of Compliance para 72; Counter-Fraud
  and Cyber Security Frameworks; branch staff awareness every three months.
- **Qatar:** QCB AML/CFT Instructions s 21.2-21.3 (all staff at least yearly, role-tailored); QCB Technology Risks
  Regulation 2.2 (induction and yearly awareness; board cyber training; critical incidents to QCB within 1 hour); QCB
  Data Handling and Protection Regulation 4.7, 13.2.3.
- **Bahrain:** CBB Module OM cyber training (OM-5.5.54-56); HC director induction; FP CPD (15 hours a year). The AML
  staff-training chapter (FC-6) sits on the blocked Rulebook host, so AML training is held at competence.
- **Canada:** PCMLTF Regulations s 156(1)(d)-(e) (written ongoing compliance training program); Bank Act s 627.02
  (consumer-protection training for all staff); OSFI E-21 and B-13; Canada Labour Code harassment training (within 3
  months of hire and at least every 3 years).
- **South Africa:** FIC Act s 42-43 and s 62 (training of employees); General Laws Amendment Act 22 of 2022
  (proliferation financing); Joint Standard 2 of 2024 (annual cyber awareness); PA Directive 1 of 2026 (director
  training); FAIS Fit and Proper (role-based); Protected Disclosures Act s 6(2)(a).

## Recent changes recorded in the maps (selection)

- India: RBI consolidated its circulars into bank-type Directions (Nov 2025); new cyber, fraud, compliance and audit
  Directions (31 July 2026); Integrated Ombudsman Scheme 2026; recovery-agent certification from 1 January 2027.
- Canada: Strong Borders Act (26 March 2026) — effective compliance programs, penalties up to $20 million per
  violation or 3% of global income; new Bank Act fraud-training duty passed but not yet in force.
- Malaysia: AMLA amended (Act A1761, 1 March 2026) — duties bind directors, officers and employees personally.
- Bangladesh: Personal Data Protection Act 2026, Cyber Security Act 2026, Deposit Protection Act 2026.
- Saudi Arabia: Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules (Dec 2025); PF in business risk assessment guidance.
- South Africa: removed from the FATF grey list (24 October 2025).
- Indonesia: POJK 8/2023 replaced POJK 12/2017; PADK 1/2026 on bank IT from 1 March 2026.
