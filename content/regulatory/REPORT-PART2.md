## Changes found while writing (now noted in the maps)

- United Kingdom: the Information Commission replaced the ICO on 30 September 2026 (SI 2026/1015).
- United Kingdom: FCA rules on non-financial misconduct (bullying, harassment) apply from 1 September 2026; CP26/37
  proposes replacing the Conduct Rules training duty with a notification duty (proposal only, s.64B still in force).
- United States: the April 2026 Regulation B amendment (effective 21 July 2026) removed the disparate-impact
  "effects test" from Regulation B; other federal and state fair-lending laws may still apply.
- Australia: CPG 230 was superseded on 30 June 2026; CPS 230 amendments apply from 1 July 2026.
