## Corrections found while writing (applied)

- Canada: the 2026 AML amendments come from the Strengthening Canada's Immigration System and Borders Act
  (S.C. 2026, c. 4), not a "Strong Borders Act" (Part 3 report corrected).
- Bangladesh map: PEP and correspondent relationships need the CAMLCO's approval (BFIU Circular 26 paras 3.10, 3.13).
- Bahrain map: note added on the FC-6 training chapter (2021 consultation copy; current text unconfirmed).
